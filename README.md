### What Crawlbench does

Crawlbench watches Facebook Marketplace on a schedule, runs every new listing through your filters, and pings you by email or Telegram when something matches your buy box. You open Messenger while the listing is still fresh.

Depth on one source, not a thin layer across many marketplaces.

### Built for people who buy to resell

- **Advanced filters:** price, location, keywords, exclude terms, and category-specific fields
- **Multi-city coverage:** monitor many metros without babysitting tabs
- **Match alerts:** email and Telegram ([@CrawlbenchAlertsBot](https://t.me/CrawlbenchAlertsBot))
- **Team workspaces:** one buy box and one match queue for the whole desk
